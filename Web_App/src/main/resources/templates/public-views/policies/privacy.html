Privacy Policy

One To One handles personal data to deliver coaching, scheduling, payments, and verified trainer operations. This page summarises the current privacy commitments for the platform.

Privacy Policy Payments Policy Terms of Service Subscription Terms
01

What we collect

  • Account data such as name, email address, phone number, and profile details.
  • Training data such as goals, sessions, schedules, check-ins, and trainer relationships.
  • Billing data needed to manage in-platform subscriptions and payment records.
02

Why we use it

  • To provide the coaching and client-management services inside the platform.
  • To verify trainers, support bookings, and maintain secure payment operations.
  • To protect the service, investigate abuse, and meet legal or compliance obligations.
03

Privacy commitments

  • Payments stay in-platform.
  • Access is role-based and limited to the minimum needed for each workflow.
  • Data handling is designed around GDPR and user privacy requirements.
