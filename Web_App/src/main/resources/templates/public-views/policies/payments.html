Policy

Payments must stay in-platform

ONE TO ONE protects both clients and trainers by keeping payments, booking, and communication in one secure system. Sharing off-platform payment details is not allowed and may lead to moderation actions.

Why this matters

  • Verified payment records protect both parties.
  • In-platform transactions unlock support and dispute resolution.
  • Compliance keeps the community safe and trusted.

Examples of blocked content

  • Bank transfers, sort codes, account numbers, IBAN details.
  • Third-party payment handles or links (e.g., PayPal.me).
  • Requests to meet and pay with cash.
