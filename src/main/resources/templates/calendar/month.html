Display Modes

Display Modes

Your calendar can display tasks and workouts in two different formats:

• Detailed Mode

Shows all tasks and workouts in a single, combined list with full details including time, completion status, and notes.

• Grouped Mode

Organizes tasks and workouts into separate categories for cleaner visual separation and focused viewing.

Change your display preference: Visit your profile settings and look for the "Calendar display" section.
