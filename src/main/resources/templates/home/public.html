How are trainers verified?

Quality is our firewall. Every "Pro" trainer undergoes a manual review of their certifications (CIMSPA, REPs, or equivalent) and liability insurance. We reject over 40% of applicants to ensure you only work with legitimate experts.

How do payments work?

Securely. Payments are processed via Stripe and held in escrow. Your trainer is paid only after they deliver your promised coaching block or session. This "Deployment Protection" ensures you get exactly what you pay for.

Can I switch trainers?

Yes. You are never locked into a long-term contract unless you specifically agree to one. You can switch coaches at the end of any billing cycle (usually monthly) without penalty.

What data is stored / GDPR?

We operate on a privacy-first architecture. Your health data, progress photos, and chat logs are encrypted at rest. We are fully UK-GDPR compliant and will never sell your personal performance metrics to advertisers.

Do I need a subscription?

The platform itself is free for clients. You can create an account, browse verified trainers, and read articles at no cost. You only pay the specific rate set by a coach when you hire them.

Is this UK-first?

Yes. One To One is built in the UK, for the UK fitness ecosystem. All transactions are in GBP, and our verification standards are aligned with British governing bodies like CIMSPA.
